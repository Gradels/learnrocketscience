Learn Rocket Science

Learn Rocket Science is an introductory course to the fascinating physics and maths behind how rocket navigation and astrodynamics work. This course deep-dives into how rockets work, how planets work, and how they both coincide. In this course are interactive quizzes and articles to help you get a better understanding of how this branch of physics works.
